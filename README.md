# Kilimanjaro Village Waterfalls & Culture Tour 🌍🏔️

Karibu! / Welcome! This repository contains the official information and program for the **Kilimanjaro Village Waterfalls & Culture Tour** located in Marangu, Kilimanjaro, Tanzania.

## 📌 Kuhusu Project Hii (About The Project)
Discover the hidden beauty of Marangu! This tour program is designed to give visitors an authentic experience of the Chagga culture, breathtaking waterfalls, and the natural beauty of Mount Kilimanjaro's slopes. 

*(Gundua uzuri uliofichika wa Marangu! Programu hii ya utalii imeundwa kumpa mgeni uzoefu wa kweli wa utamaduni wa Wachagga, maporomoko ya maji ya kupendeza, na uzuri wa asili wa miteremko ya Mlima Kilimanjaro.)*

## ✨ What We Offer (Tunachokitoa)
*   **Village Waterfalls:** Hike to the beautiful hidden waterfalls of Marangu.
*   **Chagga Culture:** Learn about the local traditions, dances, and daily life.
*   **Chagga Cave:** Explore the historical caves used by the Chagga people.
*   **Coffee Tours:** Experience the process of making local Kilimanjaro coffee from bean to cup.
*   **Local Food:** Taste authentic traditional Chagga meals.

## 🗺️ The Tour Program (Ratiba ya Safari)
1.  **Morning:** Pick up from your hotel in Moshi/Arusha and drive to Marangu.
2.  **Mid-Morning:** Start the hike to the waterfalls and visit the Chagga caves.
3.  **Afternoon:** Coffee tour and traditional Chagga lunch.
4.  **Evening:** Return to your hotel.
